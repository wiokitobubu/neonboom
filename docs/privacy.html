Privacy Policy

Neon Boom: Merge Puzzle · Android · Effective 14 November 2026

In short

Neon Boom has no accounts and no sign-up. Your scores and settings stay on your phone. The game shows ads from Google AdMob. To show those ads, Google collects some device data, as explained below. In Europe, the UK and some US states, the game asks for your choice first, and you can change it at any time in Settings → Privacy & ad choices.

Who we are

Neon Boom ("the game", "we") is an independent game published on Google Play. You can contact us using the developer e-mail address shown on the Neon Boom page in Google Play, or by opening an issue at github.com/wiokitobubu/neonboom/issues.

What stays on your phone

The game saves the following only on your device, so you can continue where you left off:

  • your best score, game statistics, discovered cores and settings (sound, music, vibration);
  • the pilot name you type in Settings (optional);
  • your current run, your Neon League division and the simulated league table.

We do not receive this data and we have no server. The Neon League rivals are simulated (AI) players generated on your phone; no data about you is sent to other players.

Android may include this saved game data in your personal device backup to your Google account (Android Auto Backup), under Google's control and settings. You can delete it at any time with Settings → Reset all progress in the game, or by uninstalling the game.

Your choices. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, or in a US state with its own privacy law, a Google consent message asks for your choice before personalised ads are shown. You can change that choice at any time in the game: Settings → Privacy & ad choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads depending on your phone).

Rewarded ads (for example "Continue" at game over) are always optional: you choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game bonus.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads.
  • Advertising ID (added by Google Mobile Ads): as described above.
  • Vibration: for the optional haptic feedback, which you can switch off in Settings.

The game does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, files, precise location or any account.

Children

Neon Boom is a general-audience game not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are limited to content rated for general audiences with parental guidance. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help.

Keeping and deleting data

Data saved on your phone stays until you reset your progress or uninstall the game. Data collected by Google for advertising is kept according to Google's policies; you can manage it at myadcenter.google.com.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Because we hold no personal data on any server, most requests are best handled by Google for ad data (see the links above). You can always contact us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
